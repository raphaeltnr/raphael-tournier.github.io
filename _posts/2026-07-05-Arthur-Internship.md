---
title: "Evolution of African ichthyofaunas and hydrographic dynamics"
date: 2026-07-05
---

This month I co-supervised Arthur Melun's Licence 3 internship on the evolution of African ichthyofaunas and hydrographic dynamics, alongside Olga Otero and Laurent Husson.

The internship aimed to connect the physiographic dynamics from our paleogeographic reconstructions to the evolution of African ichthyofaunas, using the traces and locations of fossil sites.
