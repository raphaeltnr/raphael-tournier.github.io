FlowScape studies the physiographic connectivity of the environment through time, via spatial analysis of drainage basin captures and regressions over time, represented using Sankey diagrams.

This connectivity is then applied biologically, using localized indices to investigate potential origins and dispersal pathways of species.

## Methods

Calibration of the effective size of drainage basins and of their exchanges. Exchanges are then polarized through time and visualized using Sankey diagrams and associated maps.
