The goal of MINOTAUR is to quickly generate climate fields informed by paleogeography and CO2 levels.

To do so, it uses a Mesh-Informed Neural Operator methodology to predict paleoclimatic data from a given paleogeography and CO2 level.

## Methods

Compilation of high-complexity coupled climate model outputs, re-homogenized to build a consistent training corpus. Training weights are determined for the different simulations, and tested against paleogeographic, CO2, and climatic variability.
