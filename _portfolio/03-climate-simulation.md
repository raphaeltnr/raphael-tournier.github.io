Climate simulations are produced using IPSL-CM5A2, in collaboration with Pierre Sepulchre and Frédéric Fluteau, and used as boundary conditions for the landscape and habitat reconstructions in this project.

As part of this work, I produced complete and internally consistent paleogeographies, integrating my reconstructed African topography (AfriTopo) within temporally coherent paleomagnetic reference frames (PMAG).

Paleogeographies are produced at 3, 8, 14, 17, 20, 25, and 30 Ma, and interpolated between time steps using the FILM tool.

*Simulations are currently in progress.*
